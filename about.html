About

The engineer behind the products.

A closer look at how I work, what I build with, and how I got here.

My name is Shuraim Asif Iqbal. I’m a Full-Stack Product Engineer focused on building production-ready web applications with React, Next.js, Node.js, MongoDB, and modern AI APIs. I enjoy creating products that combine excellent user experience with scalable backend architecture.

I care about the whole path from a blank repo to something real people use — that means owning the database schema as much as the pixel spacing, and treating an AI API as just another service to design around, not a magic box.

I’m always building something. Currently focused on shipping products that actually solve problems, not just demos that impress in a slide deck.
